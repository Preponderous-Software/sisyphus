Sisyphus

The boulder is your software. Push it up the hill: every terrace you reach ships a release, and every release adds a module, so the boulder grows. Every module rots, slowly on its own and fast when something happens to it: a dependency drifts, a test turns flaky, an advisory lands. Rot makes the boulder heavy, and a module rotted through is down, and the town's lights go out. At the summit the next major version ships. Then the platform under it reaches its end of life.

  • Mouse: look. WASD: walk; walk into the boulder from below to push it. On a terrace, push from above to roll it back and turn it. Shift: hurry.
  • Hold E on a module to patch it. Hold R to retire it: one less thing to keep.
  • Q: sit and look at the view. V: his eyes, or back over his shoulder. F1: the controls on screen. Esc: free the mouse.
  • Drag on the left half to walk (all the way out to hurry); walk into the boulder from below to push it. Drag on the right half to look.
  • Hold Patch while looking at a module to patch it; hold Retire to retire it. Sit looks at the view; Camera switches between his eyes and over his shoulder.

The game saves every minute, when you press Save (or F5), and when you leave the page; F9 loads the last save. Saves stay in this browser on this device. Built on the Strata engine; source.
